About Envi Plus

Envi Plus is a digital farm-to-market platform connecting small and marginal farmers directly with buyers.

Our goal is to help farmers get better prices, reduce post-harvest losses and improve access to reliable markets.

At the same time, customers can access fresh, nutritious and affordable food through a direct connection with farmers.

How Envi Plus Works

  1. Farmers provide their agricultural produce.
  2. Envi Plus connects farmers with buyers.
  3. Products are collected and managed efficiently.
  4. Customers order fresh agricultural products.
  5. Products reach customers directly.
